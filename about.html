Who We Are

Three decades of dedicated service to the visually impaired community of Zambia.

Our Story

Founded in 1993, Rooted in Dignity

Mulenga Corps — the Zambia Library, Cultural & Skills Centre for the Visually Impaired — was established in 1993 to address a pressing need: the absence of accessible literacy, cultural and vocational resources for persons who are blind or partially sighted.

What began as a modest transcription effort has grown into a comprehensive institution comprising eight departments and more than 110 staff positions, serving communities across six compounds in Lusaka. Our work spans Braille and audio transcription, community radio broadcasting, rehabilitation and skills development, and administrative excellence — all directed toward one outcome: genuine independence for the people we serve.

Today, Mulenga Corps is recognised as one of Zambia’s most trusted institutions in disability-inclusive development, working closely with government agencies, cooperating partners and the communities themselves.

Our Vision

A Zambia in which every visually impaired person enjoys full literacy, cultural participation and economic independence, free from barriers to opportunity.

Our Mission

To provide accessible library and transcription services, vocational skills training, cultural programming and rehabilitation support that empower visually impaired Zambians to live with dignity and self-reliance.

Main Objectives

To expand access to Braille literacy; deliver relevant vocational and life-skills training; promote awareness through broadcasting; and strengthen institutional capacity for sustainable, community-driven impact.

Leadership & Governance

Guided by Experience, Held to Account

Mulenga Corps is led by Christopher Melenga, Executive Director, whose stewardship has guided the organisation’s growth into a multi-departmental institution serving thousands across Lusaka’s compounds.

The organisation is governed by an independent Board of Trustees, which provides strategic oversight, ensures sound financial management, and safeguards the mission and values upon which Mulenga Corps was founded.
