Our Services & Departments

Eight departments, working together to deliver literacy, culture, skills and independence.

Projects & Business Development

Designs, resources and sustains Mulenga Corps’ programmes, cultivating partnerships and income-generating initiatives that secure the organisation’s long-term impact.

Radio Station Department

Operates a community radio platform that informs, educates and advocates for the rights and inclusion of visually impaired persons across Lusaka and beyond.

Rehabilitation & Skills Development

Delivers orientation, mobility training and vocational skills programmes that build practical competencies for daily living and gainful employment.

Transcription & Library Services

Produces Braille and audio transcriptions of educational and recreational material, maintaining an accessible library for learners and the wider community.

Human Resource & Administration

Oversees recruitment, staff welfare and institutional administration, ensuring that Mulenga Corps’ 110-plus positions are resourced to serve the community effectively.

Finance Department

Manages financial planning, budgeting and accountability, safeguarding donor and public confidence in the organisation’s stewardship of resources.

Braille Production & Publishing

Produces and distributes Braille learning materials, textbooks and publications, expanding access to literacy resources across Zambia’s visually impaired community.

Community Outreach & Culture

Coordinates cultural programming and community engagement initiatives that celebrate ability, foster inclusion and connect Mulenga Corps with the compounds it serves.
