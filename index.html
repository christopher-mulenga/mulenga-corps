Who We Are

Three Decades of Building Independence

Since 1993, Mulenga Corps has stood as Zambia’s foremost institution dedicated to the literacy, cultural enrichment and vocational advancement of persons who are blind or visually impaired. Through our library and transcription services, radio station, rehabilitation programmes and skills workshops, we equip individuals with the tools to live independently and contribute meaningfully to society.

Guided by an independent Board of Trustees and led by our Executive Director, Christopher Melenga, our eight departments work in concert to deliver holistic support across six compounds in Lusaka and beyond.
